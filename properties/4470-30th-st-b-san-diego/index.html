About This Property

Built in 2016 within a gated community along North Park’s 30th Street corridor, this contemporary townhome at The Dakota offers a peaceful, private refuge in the heart of one of San Diego’s most vibrant neighborhoods. Clean architectural lines, no shared walls, and a private rooftop deck framed by palm trees and city views set it apart, with the main living spaces at the center of the layout and bedroom suites kept separate for privacy. The ground level, with direct access from the attached two-car garage, a rare find in North Park, opens to a versatile bedroom with its own full bath, well-suited for guests, a home office, or a quiet retreat away from the main living areas. Upstairs, the main living level is open and light-filled, with 12-foot ceilings, high-set windows that invite natural light and warm honey bamboo flooring throughout. The kitchen anchors the space, appointed with white shaker cabinetry, quartz countertops, a mosaic backsplash, and stainless steel appliances. A generous island with seating opens to the living room, while the dining area is defined by a drum pendant and a band of high windows that brighten the room while maintaining privacy. Under-stair storage, a hall closet, and a powder room round out this level. The top floor holds two bedroom suites, each with its own bath. The primary suite offers a dual-sink vanity topped in quartz and a frameless glass walk-in shower set against a floor-to-ceiling vertical mosaic tile wall. The second suite includes a walk-in closet and a bathroom finished in large-format grey porcelain tile with a vertical mosaic accent and walk-in shower. A laundry closet on the landing accommodates a stackable washer and dryer, keeping laundry on the same floor as the bedrooms. Above it all, the private rooftop deck functions as a true outdoor living room. Parquet-pattern deck tiles, a built-in grill, and an outdoor kitchen with sink and open shelving create an easy setting for weekend brunch, evening gatherings, or a quiet sunset, with views over the neighborhood toward palm trees and the surrounding skyline. Step outside and the best of North Park is within walking distance: the restaurants, craft breweries, coffee shops, and live music venues along 30th Street, University Avenue, and Adams Avenue, with Balboa Park close by and quick access to I-805, SR-15, and I-5.
