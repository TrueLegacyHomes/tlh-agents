The Renovation

Warm modern. Built for Newport.

This is a complete gut renovation — every room touched. The design language is warm organic-modern: pale oak cabinetry, brass hardware, zellige tile, and wood-plank ceilings that bring texture and warmth without going precious about it.

The primary suite is the standout. Fluted oak paneling, custom built-ins, and a spa-caliber bathroom with two separate vanity areas and a walk-in closet that buyers will remember. The kitchen features a custom arched niche with mauve zellige tile backsplash — the kind of detail that photographs well and holds its value.

Built for the buyer who wants something finished, distinctive, and move-in ready — without the two years of contractor headaches.
